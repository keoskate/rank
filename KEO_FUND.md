## The rules (the part that matters more than the picks)

1. **Core sleeves: inverse-volatility weights**, recomputed monthly. Calm compounders get more dollars; wild names get less. Risk is spread evenly even when conviction isn't.
2. **Band rebalancing (core only):** drift gets pulled back to target monthly — mechanically trims euphoria, buys dips, and refills equity FROM ballast at crash bottoms.
3. **VC rules (spec sleeve only):** buy once (~1.1% each). **Never top up a loser** (a falling bet is information, not a discount). **Never trim a winner** until it exceeds 5% of the fund — then sell half, let the rest ride. Power-law capture, encoded.
4. **Loose vol brake (fund level):** scale exposure toward cash only when fund vol breaches ~20% annualized — inert in normal times, engaged in storms. Backtest: +0.10 Sharpe, −4.8pts max drawdown, for ~2.6% CAGR of bull drag.
5. **Sector honesty:** semis + equipment hard-capped at ~30%. True NVIDIA-complex exposure is far larger through TSM/ASML/AVGO/VRT/CRWV — held knowingly.

## Expectations (from the characterization backtest, 2016→2026)

- **Survivorship warning:** these are hand-picked winners; absolute backtest returns (25.9% CAGR) are inflated by construction and are NOT expectations. The honest forward framing: equity-market returns with a technology tilt, and materially better crash behavior.
- **What the policy verifiably does:** holding these same names naively drew down **−41.6%** (−38% in 2022). The policy structure took the identical stocks through at **−27%** (−22% in 2022) and −6% vs −15% in 2018.
- **Pre-accepted feelings:** a 2022-type year ≈ **−22 to −26%**. In melt-up years the fund WILL badly lag naive aggression (2024: +37% vs +116% naive). Both are the seatbelt working.
